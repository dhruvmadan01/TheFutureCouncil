Role Breakdown

What a Chapter Lead does.

1. Host Monthly Build Nights

Organize low-friction evening sprints on your campus where students code, design, and validate prototypes together.

2. Facilitate Co-Founder Matches

Connect engineers with commercial talent, designers with researchers, turning individual projects into venture teams.

3. Run Semester Demo Days

Give your campus builders a stage to present their traction to local founders, alumni, and investors.
