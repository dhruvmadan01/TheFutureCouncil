TFC Incubator · Applications open

The future doesn't build itself.

We back the founders who build it.

The TFC Incubator backs early-stage founders with government grants, an investor network, mentorship and a community of top founders. We take 0% equity.

What you get

Everything an early founder needs, at 0% equity.

Grants

Access to government grants of up to ₹10 lakh, so you can start building before you raise.

Investors

Introductions to a network of angel investors and family offices with ₹1,000 Cr in combined capital.

Mentorship

Operators who help you build, ship and raise.

Community

A network of top founders to build alongside.
