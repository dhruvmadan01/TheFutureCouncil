Zero-to-One Validation Framework

OPEN COMMUNITY RESOURCE

1. The 5-Question Customer Litmus Test

Never ask "Would you buy this?" (people lie to be nice). Instead, ask about past behavior:

  • "What's the hardest part about [doing X] today?" (Reveals top-of-mind pain)
  • "When was the last time you encountered that problem?" (If not within 14 days, not urgent)
  • "What have you tried to solve it so far?" (If no DIY solutions tried, won't pay)
  • "How much time or money does this problem currently cost you?" (Quantifies value)
  • "Who else should I talk to who deals with this?" (Viral discovery loop)

2. The 48-Hour Demand Test

Before coding, set up a simple one-page site with an explicit "Join Private Beta" button. If you cannot get 15 strangers to sign up from direct conversations, building product won't solve distribution.
