3. Startup School Subscription Terms

Startup School is offered as a monthly recurring subscription at ₹399/month (inclusive of all applicable taxes), cancel anytime. Subscribers receive access to all 9 practical curriculum modules and the weekly Sunday Live Lab (Sundays 6:00–7:30 PM IST) with visiting startup operators. You may cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation penalties by emailing support@thefuturecouncil.in. The founder Resource Library is free for everyone.

4. Launchpad Fellowship Terms & 0% Equity

Launchpad is open to student founders and early-stage builders from any college or university across India. Launchpad selects 20 founders per cohort for an intensive 4-week fully online fellowship ending in Demo Day.

  • 0% Equity: The Future Council and TFC Innovations Pvt. Ltd. do not take any equity, shares, warrant rights, or advisory equity in your venture. You retain 100% founder ownership.
  • Application Fee: There is a one-time, non-refundable application fee of ₹999. This fee covers dedicated evaluation and individualized written feedback (including 2–3 actionable points to fix) from our review team. Fee waivers available if ₹999 is a financial barrier. (Request a fee waiver)
  • Unselected Applicant Guarantee: If your application is not selected for the 20-founder cohort, you receive personalized written feedback with 2–3 specific fixes, 1 free month of Startup School (worth ₹399), and a 50% discount code (₹499 fee) valid for the subsequent Launchpad cohort application.

5. The 5% Success Fee Clause

In alignment with our "We win only when you win" principle, The Future Council charges a one-time cash success fee of 5% on capital raised by the founder's company, subject strictly to the following conditions:

  • The fee applies solely to capital raised directly from investors, funds, or angel syndicates that The Future Council personally introduced to the founder.
  • The capital must be officially committed and received within 12 (twelve) months following the cohort's Demo Day.
  • If the founder raises capital independently from their own contacts, or if the venture does not raise capital, no success fee is owed (₹0).
