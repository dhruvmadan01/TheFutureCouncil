3. Launchpad Accelerator Terms & 2% Equity

Launchpad is open to student founders and early-stage builders from any college or university across India. Launchpad selects 20 founders per cohort for an intensive 4-week fully online startup accelerator cohort ending in Demo Day.

  • 2% Equity Alignment: Launchpad takes 2% equity in the 20 selected cohort ventures under a standard founder agreement. This aligns our skin in the game directly with your venture's long-term growth and fundraising. Founders retain 98% ownership.
  • Application Fee: There is a one-time, non-refundable application fee of ₹999. This fee serves to attract serious candidates, eliminate automated submissions, and cover dedicated evaluation and individualized written feedback from our review team for every applicant.
  • Unselected Applicant Access: If your application is not selected for the 20-founder cohort, you receive personalized written feedback and full access to attend all virtual leader and mentor sessions throughout the cohort. (Founder pods, 1:1 mentor office hours, investor introductions, and Demo Day pitch slots remain exclusive to the 20 selected cohort founders.)

5. No Success Fees on Capital Raised

The Future Council charges 0% success fees (₹0) on capital raised by founders. Our partnership is rooted solely in the 2% equity alignment for selected cohort companies. Whether you raise institutional funding through our introductions or independently, you never owe any cash success fees to The Future Council.
