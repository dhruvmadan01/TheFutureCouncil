3. Launchpad Terms

Launchpad is a free community service run by The Future Council for student founders and early-stage builders from any college or university. Applications are open any time. Applicants are interviewed by our team, and we select founders based on the interview.

  • No Equity: Launchpad takes no equity in your startup. Founders retain 100% ownership.
  • No Application Fee: Applying and being interviewed are free. Launchpad charges no fee at any stage.
