3. TFC Incubator Terms & Fees

The TFC Incubator is open to early-stage founders. Applications are reviewed by our team, and not every applicant is selected.

  • No Program Fee: The TFC Incubator charges no program fee and no tuition. The only payment is the one-time ₹999 application fee. The TFC Incubator takes 0% equity, and founders retain 100% ownership.
  • Application Fee: There is a one-time, non-refundable application fee of ₹999. This fee serves to attract serious candidates, eliminate automated submissions, and cover dedicated evaluation and individualized written feedback from our review team for every applicant.
  • Feedback: If your application is not selected, you receive personalized written feedback on your application.

4. No Success Fee for Founders

The Future Council charges founders no success fee (₹0) on capital raised. TFC takes 0% equity in TFC Incubator companies. Whether you raise funding through our introductions or independently, you never owe a success fee to The Future Council.
