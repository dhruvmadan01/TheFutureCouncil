2. Program Application & Registration Fee

To apply for the Fellowship '26 cohort, applicants must complete the application form and pay a non-refundable Application Registration Fee of ₹2,000 (Rupees Two Thousand Only).

Payment of the application fee does not guarantee admission or selection into the fellowship cohort. Selection is strictly merit-based, evaluating prototype viability, problem depth, and founder screening interviews.

3. Program & Application Fee Policy

  • Program Fee: The Fellowship program fee is ₹0 (100% grant-funded and equity-free; strictly limited to only 20 founders in total). Selected fellowship founders do not pay any tuition fees to participate in the cohort.
  • Application Fee: The application registration fee of ₹2,000 is charged to ensure high-intent applications from serious builders and filter out spam. It is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances, including non-selection of your application, withdrawal, or scheduling conflicts.

4. Program Commitments & Offline Attendance

Fellowship '26 is an offline intensive cohort. While it is not a residential program, selected members are required to visit designated partner hubs in Delhi NCR for workshops, pitch clinics, founder mixers, sprints, and Demo Day evaluations. Participants must ensure availability for these sessions. Accommodation location guidance is provided, but living costs are borne entirely by the participant.
