MEMBERSHIP · FREE, ALWAYS

Free to join. Everything to gain.

Joining the Council costs nothing and takes two minutes. You get into builder vaults, project squads, campus mixers, and first access to the Fellowship application — whether you already have a startup or you're still looking for the people to build one with.

WHAT MEMBERSHIP GETS YOU
01

Builder Vaults

Digital spaces where student founders, engineers, and designers find each other before an idea even has a name.

02

Project Squads

Get matched into a working team instead of cold-messaging strangers on LinkedIn.

03

Campus Mixers

In-person events at your chapter, and access to Delhi NCR mixers even if your campus doesn't have one yet.

04

Fellowship Priority

Members get first access when Fellowship applications open each cohort.

WHO SHOULD JOIN

Any student builder, engineer, designer, or operator — whether you've already got a startup running or you're still looking for your team. There's no minimum experience, no application fee, and no requirement that you already have an idea.
