How You Can Engage

Six high-impact ways to guide founders.

Pick the format that fits your busy schedule. We handle scheduling, agendas, and pod coordination.

01

1:1 Tactical Office Hours

Host 30-minute drop-in problem-solving sessions with founders facing concrete architecture, distribution, or hiring roadblocks.

02

Founder Pod Advisor

Serve as a sounding board for an intimate pod of 4–5 founders building in your vertical throughout the 4-week cohort.

03

Sunday Live Labs

Lead a 45-minute interactive masterclass or live teardown on product discovery, GTM playbooks, or cloud infrastructure.

04

Pitch & Deck Roasts

Sharpen founder storytelling, metric clarity, and unit economic defenses before they pitch live angels at Demo Day.

05

Angel Co-Investment

Get a direct first-look opportunity to angel invest early in breakout founders you've mentored with zero platform fees.

06

Council Network

Connect with a curated community of fellow VPs, senior engineering managers, product leaders, and exited startup founders.
