Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 · Operating Entity: TFC Innovations Pvt. Ltd.

1. Launchpad Application Fee Policy

The Launchpad Fellowship application fee is ₹999 (one time). Because each application triggers an immediate, hands-on review process conducted by our team—resulting in customized written feedback outlining strengths and actionable fixes—the application fee is non-refundable once submitted.

To ensure fairness and accessibility:

  • Fee Waivers: Fee waivers available if ₹999 is a financial barrier. Any applicant facing financial hardship may request a fee waiver by writing to support@thefuturecouncil.in prior to submitting payment.
  • Unselected Applicant Guarantee: If your application is not selected for the 20-founder cohort, you are automatically issued:
    • Written feedback with 2–3 specific fixes
    • 1 free month of Startup School (worth ₹399)
    • A 50% discount code (₹499 fee) valid for the subsequent Launchpad cohort application.

2. Startup School Subscription Cancellations & Refunds

Startup School is billed on a monthly recurring basis at ₹399/month. You may cancel anytime by emailing support@thefuturecouncil.in.

Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period, with no subsequent charges. If you were billed due to an accidental auto-renewal, you may request a full refund within 48 hours of the charge by writing to support@thefuturecouncil.in.

3. Collegiate Chapters

Joining and participating in a collegiate chapter of The Future Council is 100% free of charge. There are no fees or billing terms associated with campus chapter membership.

4. Duplicate Payments & Processing Errors

In the event of technical errors resulting in duplicate debits or unverified UPI transactions, we will refund duplicate or erroneous payments to the original payment source within 5–7 business days of receiving the transaction details at support@thefuturecouncil.in.

5. 5% Success Fee on Introduced Capital

As outlined in Section 5 of our Terms of Service, The Future Council takes 0% equity. A 5% success fee applies exclusively to capital officially committed and received within 12 months following Demo Day from investor introductions directly facilitated by The Future Council. If you raise capital independently from your own network or do not raise external capital, no success fee is owed (₹0).

6. Contact Us

For billing questions or refund inquiries, please email our support team at support@thefuturecouncil.in with your registered email and transaction reference.
