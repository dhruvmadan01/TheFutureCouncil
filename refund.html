Cancellation & Refund Policy

[NOTE FOR LEGAL REVIEW]: This policy reflects The Future Council restructure brief (Spec Section 3 & 5.10): (1) The non-refundable ₹1,999 Launchpad application fee covering individual written feedback; (2) 50% discount on reapplication (₹999) and 1 month free Startup School for unselected applicants; (3) Transparent cancellation terms for Startup School (₹399/mo).

1. Launchpad Application Fee Policy

The Launchpad Fellowship application fee is ₹1,999 (one time). Because each application triggers an immediate, hands-on review process conducted by our team—resulting in customized written feedback outlining strengths and actionable fixes—the application fee is non-refundable once submitted.

To ensure financial fairness:

  • Fee Waivers: Any applicant facing genuine financial hardship may request a fee waiver directly on the application form prior to payment.
  • Unselected Applicant Guarantee: If your application is not selected for the 20-founder cohort, you are automatically issued:
    • 1 free month of Startup School (worth ₹399)
    • A 50% discount code (₹999 fee) valid for the subsequent Launchpad cohort application.

2. Startup School Subscription Cancellations & Refunds

Startup School is billed on a monthly recurring basis at ₹399/month. You may cancel your subscription at any time via your account settings or by emailing support@thefuturecouncil.in.

Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period, with no subsequent charges. If you were billed due to an accidental auto-renewal, you may request a full refund within 48 hours of the charge by writing to support@thefuturecouncil.in.

3. Collegiate Chapters

Joining and participating in a collegiate chapter of The Future Council is 100% free of charge. There are no fees or billing terms associated with campus chapter membership.

4. Duplicate Payments & Processing Errors

In the event of technical errors resulting in duplicate debits or unverified UPI transactions, refunds will be initiated automatically to the original payment source within 5–7 business days upon receipt of transaction details.

5. Contact Us

For billing questions or refund inquiries, please email our support team at support@thefuturecouncil.in with your registered email and transaction reference.
