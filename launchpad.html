Everything built for momentum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What does Launchpad cost?
There is a one-time application fee of ₹999. If you are selected, there are no additional tuition or enrollment fees. Fee waivers available if ₹999 is a financial barrier (Request a fee waiver).
Is admission competitive?
Yes. Each cohort is strictly capped at 20 founders. We select based entirely on the problem you are solving, your tangible progress, and your team's agency. We never select based on college prestige or GPA.
Is Launchpad really 100% equity-free?
Yes, 0% equity, forever. We never take shares, warrants, or advisory rights in your company.
How does the 5% success fee work?
If (and only if) you raise institutional funding or angel capital from investors that The Future Council personally introduced you to within 12 months of Demo Day, you pay a one-time 5% cash success fee on the money raised. If you raise independently from your own contacts, or if you don't raise, you owe us absolutely nothing (₹0).
What happens if I am not selected?
Every applicant receives personalized written feedback outlining what was strong and 2–3 actionable areas to improve. In addition, you get 1 free month of Startup School (worth ₹399) and a 50% discount code (₹499 fee) for your next Launchpad application.
What is the weekly time commitment?
About 6 to 8 hours per week. This includes our weekly Sunday Live Lab, mentor office hours, and pod check-ins. The schedule is designed specifically around student college classes and exams.
Do I need a registered company or DPIIT recognition to apply?
No. You can apply with a working prototype, early user interviews, or an active side project. We help you with company incorporation and DPIIT recognition during the program if and when it makes sense.
