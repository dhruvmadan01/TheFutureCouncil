Not selected? You still leave with something

Every applicant gets written feedback on what to improve.

Do I need a startup idea to apply?

No. You can apply with an idea or without one. If you have an idea, you tell us about it in the application. If you don't, you skip that part and Launchpad helps you find a problem worth solving in Week 1.

What does it cost?

A one-time application fee of ₹99. The program fee is ₹1,50,000, but it is 100% waived, so Launchpad costs ₹0 for everyone. The only thing you pay is the application fee, and applicants who are not selected are never charged the program fee. TFC takes 0% equity and charges no success fee.

Do I need to be in Delhi NCR?

Yes, for Cohort 02. It runs in person in Delhi NCR, so you need to be able to attend the sessions there. Cohort 01 (Winter) also runs in person in Delhi NCR.

Do I need to be in a Chapter?

No. Anyone can apply, whichever college you're at, or even if you're not in college.

How competitive is it?

Twenty founders are selected per cohort. We look at what you've built and learned, not your college or your grades.

What if I'm not selected?

You get written feedback on what to improve.

How much time does it take?

Plan for 6 to 8 hours a week, in person in Delhi NCR.

Do you help with careers too?

Yes. TFC has partnered with Fire in the Belly (FTB) for internship support, for members whose startup doesn't work out and for those who don't start one.

When do cohorts start?

There are four cohorts a year, named by season. Cohort 01 (Winter) kicks off on 1 November. Registrations for Cohort 02 (Spring) are open until 15 January 2027, and it kicks off on 1 February 2027.
