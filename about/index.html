The Founders' Note

Dhruv Madan & Aryaveer Chauhan
Founders, The Future Council

Why we started The Future Council.

We're students too. We watched friends spend three years preparing for one placement season, and a handful spend the same years building something of their own.

The students in the placement queue were memorizing answers to mock interviews, stressing over CGPA cut-offs, and waiting for someone else to pick them. The handful who were building had real users, learned how to sell, and gained genuine confidence.

We started The Future Council so that the second path wouldn't depend on which college you got into. If you have the agency to build something real, you should have access to co-founders, operator mentorship, and investor capital from day one.

Principles

Our Core Values

How we make decisions, run cohorts, and treat student founders.

Value 01

Access Over Pedigree

Fully online, generous fee waivers, and written feedback for every applicant. We evaluate what you have shipped, never your college brand or GPA.

Value 02

Build Over Talk

No business plan competitions or theoretical pitch deck debates. We measure live prototypes, user interviews, and customer traction.

Value 03

We Win When You Win

Zero percent equity, ever. Our real upside is a one-time 2% success fee only on capital raised from investors we personally introduce.

Value 04

Community Before Competition

Campus chapters, peer pods of 4–5 founders, and an open alumni network. We help each other debug, test, and distribute.

Value 05

Honest About Risk

Most startups fail. We don't sell illusions. We teach the fundamentals and highlight deferred placement safety nets so students build with confidence.

Value 06

Builders Pay It Forward

Today's student fellows become tomorrow's campus chapter leads, mentor operators, and angel investors for the next cohort.
