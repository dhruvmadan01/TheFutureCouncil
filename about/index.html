The founders' note

Why we started TFC.

Dhruv Madan & Aryaveer Chauhan

We are students too. We watched friends spend three years preparing for one placement season, and a handful spend the same years building something of their own.

The students in the queue memorized mock-interview answers, stressed over CGPA cut-offs and waited for someone else to pick them. The handful who built had real users, learned to sell and gained genuine confidence.

We started The Future Council so the second path would not depend on which college you got into. If you have the agency to build something real, you should have co-founders, operator mentorship and investors from day one.

Principles

Our core values.

Access over pedigree

Launchpad is fully online, with written feedback for every applicant. We evaluate what you have shipped, never your college brand or GPA.

Build over talk

No business plan competitions or pitch deck debates. We measure live prototypes, user interviews and customer traction.

We win when you win

We take 2% equity in selected Launchpad companies and no success fee, so our incentives sit with your long-term success.

Community before competition

Chapters, pods of 4–5 founders and fellow builders across India. We help each other debug, test and distribute.

Honest about risk

Most startups fail. We do not sell illusions. We teach the fundamentals and point to the deferred-placement safety nets.

Builders pay it forward

Founders from one cohort become mentors and angel investors for the next cohort.

Radical transparency

How we make money.

No hidden incentives. Chapters are free for every student. Launchpad takes a one-time ₹999 application fee and 2% equity in the 20 selected ventures, so founders keep 98%. There is no success fee.
