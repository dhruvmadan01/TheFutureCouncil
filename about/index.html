The founders' note

Why we started The Future Council.

We are students. We watched friends spend years preparing for placements while a few others built their own companies. We started TFC so the builder path is open to everyone, whichever college they attend, with co-founders, operator mentorship and investor capital from day one.

Dhruv Madan and Aryaveer Chauhan

How we work

The values behind how we make decisions, run cohorts and treat student founders.

Access over pedigree

Open to every college, with written feedback for every applicant. We judge what you've shipped, not your college or GPA.

Build over talk

Live prototypes, user interviews and traction count. Business plan contests don't.

We win when you win

2% equity in selected ventures and zero success fee. Our incentives follow yours.

Community before competition

Campus Chapters, peer pods of 4 to 5 founders and an alumni network.

Honest about risk

Most startups fail. We teach the fundamentals and point to the deferred-placement safety nets.

Builders pay it forward

Today's fellows become tomorrow's Chapter leads, mentors and angels.

Radical transparency on money

You should know exactly what TFC earns, before you apply.
