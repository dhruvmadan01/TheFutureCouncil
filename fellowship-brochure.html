Got an idea? Bring it.

Fellowship '26 is engineered for early-stage student founders ready to turn raw vision into validated products. Over eight weeks in Delhi NCR, you receive hands-on operator advisory, technical masterclasses, and weekly teardowns from founders who've scaled from 0 to 1 — plus active assistance recruiting co-founders, full-stack engineers, and product designers from our collegiate chapters.

04 · Syllabus Roadmap

The 8-Week Build: Zero to One

Orientation kicks off on Monday, Oct 19. Every subsequent week follows our structured rhythm: two build-focused weekday sessions, followed by a Saturday leader masterclass, closed-door AMA, and teardown sprint.

Week 1 Problem Validation & Customer Discovery
Oct 20–25
Tue / Fri
Customer discovery & hypothesis formulation — Drafting falsifiable customer assumptions and non-leading interview questions; live 40-target customer outreach sprint; conducting user interviews to extract hard truths.
Saturday
Validating real market demand before writing code — Seed-Funded Founder Masterclass; live hypothesis canvas teardowns with actionable operator critique.
Week 2 Grassroots Talent & Value Proposition
Oct 26–Nov 1
Tue / Fri
Synthesizing discovery into a high-resonance value proposition; pitching technical builders and sourcing co-founders; founder role definitions, equity split clinics, and founder vesting schedules.
Saturday
Co-founder equity splits, vesting & avoiding early breakups — Serial Founder Masterclass; grassroots talent matchmaking mixer with engineers and designers from DU/IIT chapters.
Week 3 Rapid Prototyping & Flow Elimination
Nov 2–8
Tue / Fri
The 1-flow PRD — Radically scoping an MVP to one core action; Figma wireframing for non-designers; peer-to-peer usability testing and edge-case elimination.
Saturday
Building intuitive zero-to-one UX without bloated feature sets — Head of Product Masterclass; on-screen interactive prototype teardowns.
Week 4 The 7-Day MVP Build Sprint
Nov 9–15
Tue / Fri
Rapid full-stack architecture & database schema reviews; instrumenting product analytics from day zero (PostHog/Mixpanel); setting up staging environments and CI/CD pipelines.
Saturday
Deploying resilient MVPs & dodging cloud infrastructure traps — Lead Systems Architect Masterclass; live staging deployment audit for all 20 products.
Week 5 Launch & Distribution Engine
Nov 16–22
Tue / Fri
High-conversion launch copywriting for LinkedIn, X, Reddit, and student communities; concierge onboarding protocols for your first 25 organic users.
Saturday
Zero-CAC growth: community seeding & viral campus loops — Growth Operator Masterclass; co-launch sprint, pushing all 20 founder products live to real users.
Week 6 Retention, Churn & Investor Readiness
Nov 23–29
Tue / Fri
Analyzing cohort retention curves & churn triage; designing early monetization hypotheses and executing willingness-to-pay pricing experiments.
Saturday
Unit economics, sustainable burn & startup fundability — Seed Angel Operator Masterclass; investor readiness audits and metric validation.
Week 7 Pitch Storytelling & Slide Deck
Nov 30–Dec 6
Tue / Fri
The 10-slide seed pitch deck formula; data presentation, narrative arcs, and peer deck reviews; crafting compelling product demos.
Saturday
What venture and angel investors evaluate in the first 90 seconds — Active Angel Investor Masterclass; rapid-fire slide deck teardowns.
Week 8 Dress Rehearsal & Demo Day
Dec 7–19
Tue / Fri
Handling tough investor Q&A under simulated cross-examination; final deck lockdown, stage pacing, and product presentation rehearsal.
Saturday
Official Demo Day Showcase — 3-minute pitches with live investor Q&A before top angel syndicates, micro-VCs, and ecosystem leaders, followed by the formal Alumni Credential Ceremony.
