GOT AN IDEA? BRING IT.

Fellowship '26 is built for early-stage founders ready to turn a vision into a real product. Over eight weeks in Delhi NCR, you'll get hands-on mentorship, equity-free grants, and advisory from operators who've done it — plus active help finding the co-founders, developers, and designers you need. No team required to apply. No traction required either.

04

THE 8-WEEK BUILD

Kicks off Monday, Oct 19 with orientation and cohort introductions. Every week runs the same shape: two build-focused weekday sessions, then a Saturday leader masterclass, AMA, and teardown.

Week 1 · Problem Validation & Customer DiscoveryOct 20–25
  • TUE/FRICustomer discovery & hypothesis formulation — falsifiable assumptions, non-leading questions; live 40-target outreach build; interviewing users for hard truths.
  • SATValidating real market demand before writing code — seed-funded founder; hypothesis canvas teardowns.
Week 2 · Grassroots Talent & Value PropositionOct 26–Nov 1
  • TUE/FRISynthesizing discovery into a value proposition canvas; pitching builders and sourcing co-founders; role & equity clinic.
  • SATCo-founder equity splits, vesting & avoiding team breakups — serial founder; grassroots talent matchmaking mixer.
Week 3 · Rapid Prototyping & Flow EliminationNov 2–8
  • TUE/FRIThe 1-flow PRD — scoping an MVP to one core action; Figma wireframing for non-designers; peer usability testing.
  • SATBuilding addictive zero-to-one UX without bloat — head of product; on-screen prototype teardown.
Week 4 · The 7-Day MVP Build SprintNov 9–15
  • TUE/FRIRapid full-stack architecture & database schema review; product analytics from day zero (PostHog/Mixpanel).
  • SATDeploying resilient MVPs & avoiding cloud billing traps — lead systems architect; live staging deployment audit.
Week 5 · Launch & Distribution EngineNov 16–22
  • TUE/FRIOrganic launch copywriting for LinkedIn, X, Reddit and campus groups; concierge onboarding for your first 25 users.
  • SATZero-CAC growth: community seeding & viral campus loops — growth operator; co-launch sprint, all 20 founders live.
Week 6 · Retention, Churn & Micro-Grant AuditNov 23–29
  • TUE/FRIAnalyzing cohort retention & churn triage; early monetization and willingness-to-pay experiments.
  • SATUnit economics, real burn & startup fundability — seed angel operator; micro-grant milestone audit.
Week 7 · Pitch Storytelling & Slide DeckNov 30–Dec 6
  • TUE/FRIThe 10-slide seed deck formula; visual storytelling & data presentation; peer slide review.
  • SATWhat angel investors evaluate in the first 90 seconds — active angel investor; rapid pitch teardowns.
Week 8 · Dress Rehearsal & Demo DayDec 7–19
  • TUE/FRIHandling hostile investor Q&A with simulated cross-examination; stage logistics & final deck lock.
  • SATDemo Day — 3-minute pitches with live investor Q&A in front of angels, micro-VCs, and ecosystem operators, then the alumnus credential ceremony.
