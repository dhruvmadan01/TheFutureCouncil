HOW THE COHORT RUNS

The 4 pillars of Fellowship '26

01 — WEEKLY COHORT LAB

Weekly Cohort Lab

One live session a week — mentor talks, workshops, peer feedback rounds. The anchor everyone shows up for together.

02 — FOUNDER PODS

Founder Pods

Groups of 4–6 founders meet weekly outside the main call — this is where the real depth and accountability happen.

03 — MENTOR OFFICE HOURS

Mentor Office Hours

1:1 slots with operators and investors from the Future Council network, booked directly by each founder.

04 — DEMO DAY

Virtual Demo Day

A virtual pitch event in front of investors and alumni to close the cohort — treated as the flagship moment, not an afterthought.

TIMELINE (4 WEEKS)

Curriculum & Sprint Cadence

WEEK 1
Kickoff & Pod Formation

Cohort orientation, founder matching into 4-6 person pods, and benchmark goals.

WEEK 2
Live Labs & Pod Check-ins

Live labs, execution workshops, and weekly pod accountability check-ins.

WEEK 3
Demo Prep & Mock Pitches

Deck teardowns, 1:1 mentor office hours, and mock pitch feedback rounds.

WEEK 4
Virtual Demo Day

Flagship virtual demo day pitching to active investors, angels, and alumni.

WHO SHOULD APPLY

Open Admission — No Shortlist

Open to any student founder building an early-stage venture, in or outside a Future Council chapter, who can commit roughly 6–8 hours a week for 4 weeks, fully remote. There's no competitive shortlist — anyone who meets these basics and completes registration is in the cohort.
