The 9 Modules

Every module ends with real proof.

No passive video lectures. You leave every module with a concrete artifact in your hands.

Module 01

Finding a problem worth solving

Why "good ideas" often fail and painful problems win. Frameworks for evaluating hair-on-fire customer problems.

You leave with: Problem statement
Module 02

Talking to users

The Mom Test methodology. How to interview prospective users without asking if they like your idea.

You leave with: 20 interview notes
Module 03

Building an MVP with AI tools

Using modern LLM coding agents, low-code databases, and rapid frontends to ship a functioning product in days.

You leave with: Live prototype
Module 04

Your first 10 customers

Unscalable sales tactics: cold DMs, campus distribution hacks, and getting users to actually swipe their cards.

You leave with: Traction log
Module 05

Co-founders, equity & teams

How to avoid co-founder blowups. Vesting schedules, equity allocation calculators, and founder agreements.

You leave with: Founder agreement
Module 06

Money: pricing, unit economics & grants

Pricing psychology, contribution margins, and accessing non-dilutive government student grants (NIDHI, BIRAC).

You leave with: Simple model
Module 07

Company basics in India

When to incorporate (and when NOT to). DPIIT startup recognition, 80-IAC tax exemptions, and compliance basics.

You leave with: Checklist
Module 08

Startups & your college

Navigating deferred placement, attendance relaxations, academic credits, and NISP 2019 guidelines with your dean.

You leave with: Your college's options
Module 09

Pitching & fundraising

Distilling your progress into a sharp 10-slide deck and an unforgettable 2-minute pitch for angels and micro-VCs.

You leave with: Deck + 2-min pitch
