The Builder Manifesto

Don't queue. Build.

The old path is failing

Exam, seat, placements. For a growing share of students it no longer ends in a job.

A product beats a queue

Building something is a better use of college years, even if it fails.

Normal everywhere

This should be the norm at every college, not only at campuses with an incubator.

Two ways to spend four years

The skills are different. So is the proof you leave with.

Placement prep

  • Solving artificial problems
  • Pleasing interviewers
  • Simulated stakes
  • Waiting for permission

Building a startup

  • Talking to real customers
  • Shipping working products
  • Selling and distribution
  • Proof of work

Your college may already back you

Ask your dean or your T&P cell about these rights.

Deferred placement

Up to two years

Several IITs, IIIT Bangalore and several IIMs let graduating founders defer placement.

NISP 2019

Breaks and credit

The national policy asks universities for semester or year breaks, credit for startup prototypes, and up to 20% attendance relaxation for student founders.

A note for parents

Building is a serious way to learn

We know placements feel safe. We would like to show you why building a product while in college is a sound bet, whether or not the startup works.

Chapters are free to join. Several institutes have deferral safety nets, so a student who builds can still sit placements later. And the skills a founder picks up, from selling to shipping, carry into any job.

Dhruv Madan and Aryaveer Chauhan, Co-founders
