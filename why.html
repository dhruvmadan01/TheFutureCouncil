The Builder Manifesto

We were told the plan was simple. Crack the exam, get the seat, clear the placement season, and life is set.

The plan is breaking. Seats are harder to fill with offers. Machines are learning the work that entry-level jobs were made of. And a degree on its own proves less every year.

We think there's a better way to spend four years. Build something. Find a problem you can't stop thinking about, find the people who care about it too, and ship.

You might fail. Most startups do. You'll still leave college having done what no mock interview can teach: made something real, for real people, with real stakes.

This shouldn't be a privilege of a few campuses with an incubator and an alumni network. It should be normal, in every college, in every city.

That's why The Future Council exists. Don't queue. Build.

Institutional Support & Policy

The college safety nets you didn't know existed.

You don't have to choose between a degree and building a startup. Many top institutions and national policy already protect student founders.

Deferred Placement Policies

Institutions including IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Kanpur, IIT Guwahati, IIIT Bangalore, and several IIMs formally allow graduating students who start companies to defer campus placement for up to two years. If your venture doesn't work out, you return to the placement drive with full alumni support.

National Innovation & Startup Policy (NISP 2019)

Framed by the Ministry of Education (MHRD) and AICTE, the National Innovation and Startup Policy explicitly asks universities across India to allow:

  • Semester breaks and 1-year gap breaks for student founders.
  • Academic course credits for working on startup prototypes.
  • Attendance relaxation (up to 20%) for student founders pursuing customer validation and incubation.
Read the Official NISP Policy Document ↗ Ask your dean or T&P cell for these rights.
For Indian Parents

A letter to parents.

Dear parents,

We know how hard you worked to get your child into college. You want them to have financial security, dignity, and a clear path ahead.

When your child says they want to spend time building a startup, it is natural to worry. But building a startup in college is no longer a reckless gamble—it is the fastest way to become exceptionally capable.

Here is how The Future Council protects your child:

  • Zero equity taken: We do not take ownership shares in your student's work.
  • Safety nets exist: Leading colleges allow students to defer placements for up to two years. Your child does not lose their college placement rights.
  • Real skills for a changing world: Even if the startup fails, tech companies actively recruit founders because they know how to build real products, talk to customers, and solve problems independently.

College is the cheapest time in life to take an ambitious swing. Thank you for supporting their agency.

Dhruv Madan & Aryaveer Chauhan · Founders, The Future Council
