Course · Week 1 of 8

Pick a problem

Find one problem worth building a company around.

What you hand in this week

A one-page problem statement that names a specific person, the situation they are in, how they cope today and what it costs them.

Why this week matters

Everything in the next seven weeks is built on the problem you pick now, so spend this week choosing it carefully.

1. Watch and read

How to Get and Evaluate Startup Ideas, Jared Friedman, Y Combinator.

  • How to Get and Evaluate Startup Ideas ↗
    Jared Friedman, Y Combinator · Video
    Use the evaluation questions on each of your ten situations.
  • How to Get Startup Ideas ↗
    Paul Graham · Essay
    Short read. Notice his advice to look for problems you have yourself.

2. Do the work

  1. List ten frustrating situations you have seen or lived in the last month.
  2. Score each one against the four tests: specific, frequent, painful, reachable.
  3. Pick the strongest one and write the problem statement using the template.
  4. Name where you will find ten people who have this problem.

3. Use the template

Problem statement template. Copy it, fill it in, then enter your answers on your dashboard.

Who has this problem: [A specific kind of person, in a specific situation] The situation: [When does it happen, and how often?] The problem: [What goes wrong, in plain words?] How they cope today: [The workaround, tool or habit they use now] What it costs them: [Time, money or stress, with a rough number if you can] Where I will find 10 of them: [Specific places, groups or people]

4. What a pass looks like

Your submission is checked against this list. Meet every line and the week is passed.

  • Names a specific person or group, not a broad category like 'students' or 'everyone'
  • Describes a recurring situation where the problem happens
  • Describes how people cope with the problem today
  • States what the problem costs them in time, money or stress
  • Says where the founder will find at least 10 people who have this problem

Common mistakes

  • Describing a solution and calling it a problem, for example 'there is no app for X'.
  • Choosing a problem you have never seen anyone else struggle with.
  • Describing a group too broadly to be able to find and interview them.
Passing this week unlocks: Pod assignment and the community channel.
