Course · Week 4 of 8

Build a landing page

Put your idea in front of strangers and count who raises a hand.

What you hand in this week

A live landing page that collects signups or expressions of interest.

Why this week matters

A landing page lets you test your idea with strangers in a day, and the point of this week is to launch something small rather than wait until it is ready.

1. Watch and read

The Best Way To Launch Your Startup, Kat Mañalac, Y Combinator.

  • The Best Way To Launch Your Startup ↗
    Kat Mañalac, Y Combinator · Video
    Decide where you will share your page before you build it.

2. Do the work

  1. Write the headline, one-line explanation, three benefits and one call to action.
  2. Build the page and connect the signup form to a sheet you can read.
  3. Share it with at least 30 people from the right audience.
  4. Record your visit and signup counts and what people said.

3. Use the template

Landing page copy template. Copy it, fill it in, then enter your answers on your dashboard.

Headline: [Who it is for + what they get] Subheadline: [How it works, in one sentence] Three benefits: 1. 2. 3. Call to action button: [One action] Trust line: [Who is behind this and why]

4. What a pass looks like

Your submission is checked against this list. Meet every line and the week is passed.

  • A live page URL is provided that a stranger can open without logging in
  • The page states who it is for and what they get
  • The page has one clear way to sign up or show interest
  • Reports how many people visited and how many signed up
  • Describes how the page was shared and with whom

Common mistakes

  • Spending the week on design instead of getting visitors.
  • Offering several different calls to action on the same page.
  • Counting your own friends' visits and signups as demand.
