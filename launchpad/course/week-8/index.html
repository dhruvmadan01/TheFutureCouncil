Course · Week 8 of 8

Final submission

Update your numbers, polish your pitch, and tell investors who you are.

What you hand in this week

Updated traction numbers, your final pitch, and a one-page investor brief.

Why this week matters

This is your final submission, and it helps to understand how investors think about early stage companies before you write your brief.

1. Watch and read

How Startup Fundraising Works, Brad Flora, Y Combinator.

  • How Startup Fundraising Works ↗
    Brad Flora, Y Combinator · Video
    Use it to sharpen the ask in your brief.

2. Do the work

  1. Update every traction number to today and note what changed since week 6.
  2. Polish your deck and re-record your pitch if you can make it clearly better.
  3. Write your one-page brief using the template.
  4. Submit your startup name, one-liner, brief, links and ask.

3. Use the template

One-page investor brief template. Copy it, fill it in, then hand in your answers in the app.

Startup name: One-liner: Who it is for and the problem: Evidence (conversations and what you learned): Traction (numbers as of today): What changed since week 1: What I will do in the next 90 days: What I want from investors and mentors:

4. What a pass looks like

Your submission is checked against this list. Meet every line and the week is passed.

  • Provides a startup name and a clear one-line description
  • The brief explains who it is for, the problem and the evidence
  • Traction numbers are updated and stated honestly
  • States what the founder plans to do next
  • Includes working links to the final deck and the final pitch, and a specific ask

Common mistakes

  • Rounding numbers up or leaving out the ones that went down.
  • A brief that describes the product but gives no evidence.
  • Submitting links that nobody outside your team can open.
Your final submission is what investors see on Demo Day, so write it for a stranger who has never heard of your startup. Your startup name, one-liner, deck and pitch link may be shown publicly if you are chosen as a finalist.
