Course · Week 6 of 8

Get first users

Ten real people using what you built, and what they told you.

What you hand in this week

Ten real users who completed the core flow, and what you learned from them.

Why this week matters

Ten real users who completed your flow is the single strongest piece of evidence you will have, and getting them takes personal effort.

1. Watch and read

How to Get Your First Customers, Gustaf Alströmer, Y Combinator.

  • How to Get Your First Customers ↗
    Gustaf Alströmer, Y Combinator · Video
    Write a list of ten names and a message for each before you finish.

2. Do the work

  1. Write down your definition of a user before you start.
  2. Invite people personally from your week 2 interviews, your signups and the right communities.
  3. Watch each user try the core flow and write down what they say.
  4. Count how many came back, and decide the one change you will make next.

3. Use the template

User log template. Copy it, fill it in, then enter your answers on your dashboard.

Definition of a user: [What someone must do to count] For each user: Name or role: How I found them: Did they complete the flow: What they said, in their words: Did they come back: Totals: Users: Came back: The one change I will make next:

4. What a pass looks like

Your submission is checked against this list. Meet every line and the week is passed.

  • States a clear definition of a user based on completing the core flow
  • Reports at least 10 users who match that definition
  • Explains how the users were found, with real outreach rather than only friends
  • Includes real feedback from users, ideally in their own words
  • Reports how many users came back and names one concrete change planned

Common mistakes

  • Counting signups or page visits as users.
  • Only asking friends who will say yes to be polite.
  • Summarising feedback in your own words and losing the actual quotes.
Passing this week unlocks: Leaderboard listing and the investor brief template.
