Course · Week 5 of 8

Ship an MVP

Build something a stranger can use, even if it is rough.

What you hand in this week

A working link that a stranger can open and use to get the core benefit.

Why this week matters

This week you ship something rough that a stranger can use, and it is fine, even expected, for parts of it to be manual.

1. Watch and read

Tips For Technical Startup Founders, Diana Hu, Y Combinator.

  • Tips For Technical Startup Founders ↗
    Diana Hu, Y Combinator · Video
    Useful even if you do not code, for how to move fast on a first version.
  • Do Things that Don't Scale ↗
    Paul Graham · Essay
    Read it before you decide what to automate and what to do by hand.

2. Do the work

  1. Write the single flow a user must complete to get the main benefit.
  2. Build only that flow, using the fastest method available to you.
  3. Test it with three people while you watch, and fix what breaks.
  4. Record a short walkthrough and write down your success measure.

3. Use the template

MVP plan template. Copy it, fill it in, then enter your answers on your dashboard.

The one flow (start to finish): 1. 2. 3. How I will build it: [Concierge, no-code, simple code] What I am skipping on purpose: - Success measure: [What would tell me it worked] Test results (3 people): [Where they got stuck]

4. What a pass looks like

Your submission is checked against this list. Meet every line and the week is passed.

  • A working link is provided that a stranger can open and use
  • Describes the one core flow that delivers the main benefit
  • Explains how the MVP was built, including any manual steps
  • Reports testing with at least three people and what broke
  • Scope is limited to the core flow and matches the features chosen in week 3

Common mistakes

  • Building accounts, dashboards and settings before anyone has used the core flow.
  • Waiting to share until it feels finished.
  • Not testing it with anyone before sending it to real users.
Passing this week unlocks: Recorded mentor talk 2.
