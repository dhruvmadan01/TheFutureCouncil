Everything built for momentum.

No generic video lectures. Everything in Launchpad is focused on customer traction, product iteration, and investor readiness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What does Launchpad cost?
There is a one-time application fee of ₹999 to attract serious candidates, eliminate fake applications, and cover individualized written evaluation for every submission. If you are selected, there are zero tuition or enrollment fees.
Is admission competitive?
Yes. Each cohort is strictly capped at 20 founders. We select based entirely on the problem you are solving, your tangible progress, and your team's agency. We never select based on college prestige or GPA.
What is the equity model for Launchpad?
Launchpad takes 2% equity in the 20 selected cohort ventures under a clean founder agreement. This aligns our skin in the game directly with your company's long-term growth and fundraising. We charge zero success fees on capital you raise.
What happens if I am not selected for the 20-founder cohort?
Every applicant receives personalized written feedback on their venture. Furthermore, all applicants receive full access to attend all virtual leader and mentor sessions across the cohort. Founder pods, 1:1 mentor office hours, direct investor introductions, and Demo Day presentation slots remain exclusive to the 20 selected fellows.
What is the weekly time commitment?
About 6 to 8 hours per week. This includes our weekly Sunday Live Lab, mentor office hours, and pod check-ins. The schedule is designed specifically around student college classes and exams.
Do I need a registered company or DPIIT recognition to apply?
No. You can apply with a working prototype, early user interviews, or an active side project. We help you with company incorporation and DPIIT recognition during the program if and when it makes sense.
