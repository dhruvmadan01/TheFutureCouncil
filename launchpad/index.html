Launchpad · Free for student founders

The accelerator open to anyone.

A free, fully virtual program that takes you from idea to first users in eight weeks. You get the course, weekly milestones and a pod of fellow founders. Start any day and go at your own pace. Every month, up to twenty founders pitch to investors at Demo Day. No application fee. No equity. You don't need a Chapter to apply.

From sign-up to Demo Day

Everyone follows the same eight weeks. Selection happens only at the end.

Step 1

Join

Sign in with Google and pick your college. It's free and open to anyone. You start Week 1 straight away.

Step 2

Join a pod

You're placed in a pod of four to five founders, mixed across colleges where possible, with a lead who checks in every week.

Weeks 1 to 7

Build

One milestone a week, from picking a problem to your first users and a pitch deck.

Week 8

Final submission

You submit updated traction and your final pitch. Everyone who passed milestones 1 to 7 is eligible.

Selection

Rank and interview

Finalists are ranked on a rubric covering evidence of demand, quality of execution, clarity of the pitch and momentum. We interview the top 25 to 30 and choose up to 20.

Finale

Demo Day

The selected founders, the founders of the month, pitch online to investors and mentors. There is a Demo Day every month, open to everyone who has finished.

Eight milestones

Each one has a single deliverable and a clear pass or fail checklist.

Week 1

Pick a problem

A one-page problem statement: who has it, how they cope today and why it matters.

Week 2

Talk to users

Notes from 10 real customer conversations and the three insights that matter most.

Week 3

Define the solution

A one-line pitch, the core features, and what you will not build.

Week 4

Build a landing page

A live page that collects signups or interest.

Week 5

Ship an MVP

A working link that a stranger can use.

Week 6

Get first users

Ten real users and what they told you.

Week 7

Pitch deck

An 8 to 10 slide deck and a 3-minute recorded pitch.

Week 8

Final submission

Updated traction numbers and your final pitch.

Each milestone is due when the next one unlocks. Missing one doesn't remove you from the program, but you need to pass milestones 1 to 7 to be considered for Demo Day.

Built around pods

You never build alone, and you never wait for an answer from us.

Your pod

Four to five founders who joined the same month, with a shared wall to post updates, ask for help and see each other's progress.

Your own pace

Each step opens when you pass the one before. You can't skip ahead, and you're never waiting on a start date.

Leaderboard and wins

A public count of milestones passed, by person and by pod, plus a channel where founders post what they shipped.

Ask your pod first

Questions and wins go on your pod wall, where people at the same step can answer.

Chapter watch parties

Chapters can run local accountability groups around each milestone.

Instant feedback

Every submission is checked against a clear list, usually in under a minute. You see exactly what is missing and can fix it and resubmit.

Demo Day, every month

The deadline that pulls everyone through the program. See finalists and past pitches.

  • FormatOne online event every month, free for investors to attend
  • PitchersUp to 20 founders, three minutes each, followed by investor questions
  • For investorsA one-page brief on every founder, sent before the event
  • For foundersYour pitch recording and brief are kept on TFC's site
  • DateAnnounced on the Demo Day page each month

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no application fee, TFC takes no equity, and there is no success fee. Launchpad is a free community service.

Do I need to be in a Chapter?

No. Anyone can apply, whichever college you're at, or even if you're not in college.

Is it fully online?

Yes. The course, milestones, pods and Demo Day all happen online, so you can join from anywhere in India.

How are the Demo Day founders chosen?

After week 8, everyone who passed milestones 1 to 7 is ranked on a rubric. We interview the top 25 to 30 and choose up to 20. We look at what you built and learned, not your college or your grades.

What if I miss a milestone?

You stay in the program and can catch up. To be considered for Demo Day you need to pass milestones 1 to 7.

What if I'm not selected?

You get written feedback on what to improve, and you stay in the pool for the next two Demo Days.

When can I join?

Any time. There are no start dates: sign in, pick your college and start Week 1 today. Demo Day is every month, and dates are announced on the Demo Day page.

Do you help with careers too?

Yes. TFC has partnered with Fire in the Belly (FTB) for internship support, for members whose startup doesn't work out and for those who don't start one.
