Apply to Launchpad

Launchpad · Free for student founders

The accelerator open to anyone.

A free, fully virtual program that takes you from idea to first users in eight weeks. You get the course, weekly milestones and a pod of fellow founders. Twenty founders pitch to investors at Demo Day. No application fee. No equity. You don't need a Chapter to apply.

Apply to LaunchpadRun by The Future Council as a community service. Applications are open now.

The rules of the table

Plain terms. Nothing hidden.

  • Who can applyAnyone. A Chapter is not required.
  • Application feeNone
  • EquityNone
  • Success feeNone
  • FormatFully virtual. Eight weeks, one milestone a week.
  • How you progressPass each milestone by submitting proof of what you built

From sign-up to Demo Day

Everyone follows the same eight weeks. Selection happens only at the end.

Step 1

Apply

A short application. It's free and open to anyone.

Step 2

Join a pod

You're placed in a pod of four to five founders, mixed across colleges where possible, with a lead who checks in every week.

Weeks 1 to 7

Build

One milestone a week, from picking a problem to your first users and a pitch deck.

Week 8

Final submission

You submit updated traction and your final pitch. Everyone who passed milestones 1 to 7 is eligible.

Selection

Rank and interview

Finalists are ranked on a rubric covering evidence of demand, quality of execution, clarity of the pitch and momentum. We interview the top 25 to 30 and choose 20.

Finale

Demo Day

The 20 selected founders pitch online to investors and mentors.

Eight milestones

Each one has a single deliverable and a clear pass or fail checklist.

Week 1

Pick a problem

A one-page problem statement: who has it, how they cope today and why it matters.

Week 2

Talk to users

Notes from 10 real customer conversations and the three insights that matter most.

Week 3

Define the solution

A one-line pitch, the core features, and what you will not build.

Week 4

Build a landing page

A live page that collects signups or interest.

Week 5

Ship an MVP

A working link that a stranger can use.

Week 6

Get first users

Ten real users and what they told you.

Week 7

Pitch deck

An 8 to 10 slide deck and a 3-minute recorded pitch.

Week 8

Final submission

Updated traction numbers and your final pitch.

Each milestone is due when the next one unlocks. Missing one doesn't remove you from the program, but you need to pass milestones 1 to 7 to be considered for Demo Day.

Built around pods

You never build alone, and you never wait for an answer from us.

Your pod

Four to five founders and a pod lead who runs a short weekly check-in and notices when someone goes quiet.

A weekly rhythm

A new milestone unlocks on the same day every week, so the whole program moves together.

Leaderboard and wins

A public count of milestones passed, by person and by pod, plus a channel where founders post what they shipped.

Ask your pod first

Questions go to your pod, then your pod lead, then the community. The hardest ones are answered in a monthly live Q&A.

Chapter watch parties

Chapters can run local accountability groups around each milestone.

Pod leads

Alumni and strong founders lead pods and get public credit and a reference from TFC.

Demo Day

The deadline that pulls everyone through the program.

  • FormatOne online event, free for investors to attend
  • Pitchers20 founders, three minutes each, followed by investor questions
  • For investorsA one-page brief on every founder, sent before the event
  • For foundersYour pitch recording and brief are kept on TFC's site
  • DateAnnounced here with each run

What's inside

What you get when you join.

Course and templates

Short lessons, templates and checklists for every milestone, built to be used rather than watched.

Mentor talks and office hours

Recorded talks from operators who have shipped before, and booked office hours for shortlisted founders.

Investor access

Demo Day puts the strongest founders in front of investors, with a brief that makes follow-up easy.

Partner tool credits

Credits for the tools you need between idea and MVP, released as you pass milestones. Partners are being confirmed for the first run.

Builder network

A community of founders across campuses, starting with your pod.

Careers support

Internship support through our partner Fire in the Belly (FTB), even if your startup doesn't work out.

Not selected? You still leave with something

Every applicant who finishes gets written feedback on what to improve.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no application fee, TFC takes no equity, and there is no success fee. Launchpad is a free community service.

Do I need to be in a Chapter?

No. Anyone can apply, whichever college you're at, or even if you're not in college.

Is it fully online?

Yes. The course, milestones, pods and Demo Day all happen online, so you can join from anywhere in India.

How are the 20 Demo Day founders chosen?

After week 8, everyone who passed milestones 1 to 7 is ranked on a rubric. We interview the top 25 to 30 and choose 20. We look at what you built and learned, not your college or your grades.

What if I miss a milestone?

You stay in the program and can catch up. To be considered for Demo Day you need to pass milestones 1 to 7.

What if I'm not selected?

You get written feedback on what to improve and an invitation to the next run.

When can I apply?

Applications are open now. We announce the dates of each run and of Demo Day on this page.

Do you help with careers too?

Yes. TFC has partnered with Fire in the Belly (FTB) for internship support, for members whose startup doesn't work out and for those who don't start one.
