Clear Answers

Launchpad FAQ

What does it cost?
Nothing. There is no application fee, TFC takes no equity, and there is no success fee. Launchpad is a free community service for student founders.
How are founders selected?
You apply, then we interview you. We select founders based on the problem, your progress and your team, never your college.
Does TFC take equity?
No. TFC takes no equity and no success fee on anything you raise.
What if I am not selected?
You get written feedback on what's working and what to improve.
