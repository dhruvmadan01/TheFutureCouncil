Clear Answers

Fellowship '26 FAQ

Do I have to pay when submitting my application?
No payment is collected right now. Submit your application details first. Someone from our admissions team will review your project and contact you directly on WhatsApp or Email within 24 hours regarding application review, verification, and next steps.
Is admission competitive?
Yes. We select strictly 20 founders for Cohort 01 based on the problem, your progress and your team, never your college.
Does TFC take equity?
0% equity, ever. You own 100% of what you build.
How does the 2% success fee work?
We only charge a one-time 2% cash fee on funds raised from investors The Future Council introduces you to, within 12 months of Demo Day. If you raise independently or don't raise, you owe us nothing.
What if I am not selected?
Every applicant receives written feedback on what's working and 2–3 specific things to fix, 1 month of Startup School free (worth ₹399), and 50% off your next Launchpad application (₹999).
