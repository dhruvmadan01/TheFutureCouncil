Fellowship '26 FAQ

What does it cost to apply?
There is a one-time ₹999 application fee to ensure high intent, filter out fake applications, and cover individualized written feedback for every applicant. If you are accepted, there are zero tuition or enrollment fees.
Is admission competitive?
Yes. We select strictly 20 founders for Cohort 01 based on the problem, your progress and your team, never your college.
Does TFC take equity?
Launchpad takes 2% equity in the 20 selected cohort ventures under a clean founder agreement. This gives us skin in the game alongside you as long-term partners. We charge zero success fees on capital you raise.
What if I am not selected for the 20-founder cohort?
Every applicant receives personalized written feedback on what's working and specific areas to improve. In addition, you receive full access to attend all virtual leader and mentor masterclasses throughout the cohort. (Founder pods, 1:1 office hours, investor intros, and Demo Day presentation slots remain exclusive to the 20 selected cohort founders.)
