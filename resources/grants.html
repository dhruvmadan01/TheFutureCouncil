Notice board · Please read carefully

Four things that save you weeks.

Most "startup grant" lists are outdated. Read these before you fill a single form.

01

Most grants don't come from the government directly.

You apply through an approved incubator, so getting into one is often step one.

02

Many grants exclude pure software.

NIDHI-PRAYAS won't fund apps or e-commerce. Software founders should look at MeitY schemes.

03

Grants are slow.

Paperwork, a registered company and months of waiting. And none of them introduce you to investors.

04

Credits are the fastest win.

Thousands of dollars in cloud and tools within a week. No pitch, no equity.

About 6–8 hours a week, built around Sunday Live Labs, mentor office hours and pod check-ins. Everything is online.

A one-time ₹999 application fee. If you're selected, Launchpad takes 2% equity in your venture under a standard founder agreement, with no success fee on anything you raise.

What if I'm not selected?

You still get personal written feedback on your application and access to every virtual leader and mentor session during the cohort. Founder pods, 1:1 office hours, investor intros and Demo Day are for the 20 selected founders.
