The order almost every funded deck follows

Eight slides, in this order

Check your own deck against this before you open any of the ten.

  1. SLIDE 01

    Problem

    Who has it, and how painful it is.

  2. SLIDE 02

    Why now

    What changed that makes this possible today.

  3. SLIDE 03

    Solution

    What you built, in one plain sentence.

  4. SLIDE 04

    Proof

    Users, revenue, pilots or a waitlist. One real number.

  5. SLIDE 05

    Market

    How big this gets if it works.

  6. SLIDE 06

    Business model

    Who pays, how much, and how often.

  7. SLIDE 07

    Team

    Why you are the people who win this.

  8. SLIDE 08

    The ask

    How much you're raising and what it gets done.

Launchpad questions

Before you apply

Do I need a finished deck to apply?

No. Mock pitches during the cohort help you build it. You need a real problem, some progress and the will to build.

Do I need a registered company?

No. You don't need a company or DPIIT recognition to apply.

Is it only for college students?

No. It's open to student founders and early-stage builders from any college in India.

What does it cost?

A one-time ₹999 application fee. If you're selected, there's no tuition. Launchpad takes 2% equity under a standard founder agreement, with no success fee on anything you raise.

What if I'm not selected?

You still get personal written feedback and access to every virtual leader and mentor session during the cohort. Pods, 1:1 office hours, investor intros and Demo Day are for the 20 selected founders.
