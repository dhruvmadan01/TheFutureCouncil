  • Standard 1-Year Cliff: If either founder leaves within the first 12 months, 0% of equity vests.
  • Monthly Vesting Thereafter: 1/48th of total equity vests at the end of each completed month starting month 13.
  • Double-Trigger Acceleration: In case of an acquisition followed by termination without cause, 100% of unvested equity accelerates.

3. Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment

All intellectual property, proprietary software, codebase, trademark, branding, business relationships, and domains created for the company belong exclusively to the company entity upon incorporation.

4. Decision-Making & Deadlock Resolution

  • Product & Technical Architecture: Led by
  • Business, Fundraising & Hiring: Led by
  • Deadlock mechanism: If founders cannot agree on a fundamental matter (fundraising terms, pivot, or dissolution), a mutually trusted advisor (e.g. TFC Mentor / Angel) shall mediate.

5. Voluntary Departure / Bad Leaver Terms

If a founder departs voluntarily before 4 years or is removed for gross negligence / misconduct ("Bad Leaver"), unvested equity is canceled immediately, and the company maintains a right to repurchase any vested shares at fair market value or nominal price.
