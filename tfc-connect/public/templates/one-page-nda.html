One-Page Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

A concise, mutual NDA for early confidential discussions between prospective student founders, researchers, or advisors.

⚠️ TEMPLATE ONLY — NOT LEGAL ADVICE This one-page mutual NDA is intended as an informational, educational template for early discussions. While derived from standard startup practices, enforceability varies by jurisdiction. Consult a licensed legal practitioner for specific contractual counsel.

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of ("Effective Date"), by and between:

Party A: and Party B: .

1. Purpose

The parties intend to explore a potential co-founder partnership or collaborative startup venture regarding ("Purpose").

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" refers to any non-public proprietary information disclosed by either party, including trade secrets, software code, research datasets, product mockups, customer lists, and financial projections, whether disclosed in writing, orally, or electronically.

3. Obligations

  • Each party agrees to hold the other party's Confidential Information in strict confidence, exercising at least reasonable care.
  • Confidential Information shall be used exclusively for evaluating or advancing the Purpose and shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent.

4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already rightfully known prior to disclosure; or (c) was independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's information.

5. Term & Governing Law

The obligations of confidentiality shall remain in effect for a period of one (1) year from the Effective Date. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
