2. Weekly Commitment & Working Rhythm

  • Expected Hours: Each participant commits to a minimum of hours per week.
  • Daily Asynchronous Check-in: 3-bullet Slack/WhatsApp update by 10:00 AM (What I did yesterday, what I'm doing today, blockers).
  • Mid-Sprint Sync: Day 7 live demo of WIP assets (45 mins).
  • Final Retrospective: Day 14 Founder Fit Kit review & go/no-go decision on equity partnership.

3. Deliverables & Ownership Split

Track Lead Expected Sprint Output

4. Sprint IP & Exit Protocol

If at Day 14 either party decides not to proceed as co-founders:

  • Each party retains full independent ownership of whatever pre-existing code, designs, or ideas they brought into the sprint.
  • Joint code or assets created during the sprint may not be commercialized by either party without mutual written consent or a standard release.
  • No hard feelings: the goal of the trial sprint is discovering misalignment early with zero downside.

5. Post-Sprint Decision Gates

  • [ ] Gate A: Did both participants reliably hit their committed hours?
  • [ ] Gate B: How did we handle conflict, differing opinions, or setbacks?
  • [ ] Gate C: Do both feel energized to work together for the next 4+ years?
