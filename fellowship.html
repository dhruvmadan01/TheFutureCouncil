A 4-week, equity-free fellowship for student founders — now fully online.

No relocation, no campus, no single city deciding who gets in. Fellowship '26 runs entirely online this year, in a focused 4-week sprint: a weekly cohort lab, small founder pods that meet every week, direct mentor office hours, and a virtual demo day in front of real investors and alumni.

ELIGIBILITY

Who should apply

Student founders building an early-stage venture, in or outside a Future Council chapter, who can commit roughly 6–8 hours a week for 4 weeks, fully remote. We're looking for founders who want peer accountability, not just a certificate.
