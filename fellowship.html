ELIGIBILITY

Who should apply

Open to any student founder building an early-stage venture, in or outside a Future Council chapter, who can commit roughly 6–8 hours a week for 4 weeks, fully remote. There's no competitive shortlist — anyone who meets these basics and completes registration is in the cohort.
