WHO THIS IS FOR

Who This Is For

This is for founders who already have a real idea — not a pitch deck, an idea — and are ready to spend two months testing whether it's actually worth building.

You don't need a company registered, a team hired, or a product built. You need conviction and the willingness to be audited on your progress every single week.
