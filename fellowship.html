Cohort 01 Program

Fellowship '26

Launching our very first startup cohort for 20 select builder teams.

Fellowship '26 brings together 20 high-potential startup squads and independent builders from all backgrounds to build, validate, and scale. The cohort is fully open to all founders, developers, and creators, whether you are a university student or an independent builder.

FELLOWSHIP FEE & LOCATION REQUIREMENTS

Location & Fee Structure

📍 Delhi NCR Presence

Fellowship '26 is not a residential program, but you'll have to visit offline sometimes in Delhi NCR, so you need to be available for that. If you are planning to travel to Delhi NCR for this, we will help you find accommodation free of cost (though you will be responsible for the accommodation fees).

💰 Program Fee: ₹1.5 Lakhs

Need-blind admissions. We offer extensive scholarship packages for deserving builder squads.

📝 Application Fee: ₹2,000 (Payable after our team contacts you to verify details).
