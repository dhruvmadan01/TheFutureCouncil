Fellowship '26

Launching our very first startup cohort for 20 select early-stage founders.

Got an idea? Bring it. The Fellowship is built for early-stage founders ready to turn their vision into reality. We will help you build it from 0 to 1, provide hands-on mentorship, grants, and advisory, and actively help you find the right team and co-founders along the way.

Location & Fee Structure

📍 Delhi NCR Presence

Fellowship '26 is not a residential program, but you will have to visit offline sometimes in Delhi NCR, so you need to be available for that. If you are traveling to Delhi NCR for the fellowship, you will have to pay for your accommodation yourself, although we will actively help you find it.

💰 Program Fee
₹1,50,000 ₹0 100% GRANT-FUNDED

Zero tuition. The full ₹1.5L program valuation is fully grant-sponsored for the 20 selected founders — 100% equity-free.

📝 Application Fee: ₹2,000 (We charge this so we attract serious, high-intent builders rather than random applicants, making shortlisting efficient).
