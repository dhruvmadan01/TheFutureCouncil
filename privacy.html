PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026 · Transparent data security and privacy practices for all council participants.

TABLE OF CONTENTS
1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Data 3. Third-Party Sharing 4. Security & Retention 5. Cookies & Analytics 6. Contact & Rights

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you apply for our fellowship program, join a campus chapter, or register for builder updates. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, university email address, phone number, and social links.
  • Academic Affiliation: College name, course, current year of study, and campus chapter interests.
  • Founder & Project Details: Pitch descriptions, slide deck attachments, repo links, and teammate contacts.
  • Application Intent: Answers to onboarding questions regarding startup motivations and building track record.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is utilized solely to power your ecosystem experience:

  • Evaluating project applications for the Fellowship '26 cohort.
  • Generating and issuing your official verified Council Member ID card.
  • Communicating interview invites, cohort acceptance letters, and campus meetup alerts via Email and WhatsApp.
  • Administering the campus ambassador leaderboard and referral incentives.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Payments

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party ad networks. We only process data with trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Payment Processing: Fellowship application payments are processed directly through secure UPI and payment partner gateways. We do not store sensitive UPI PINs or card numbers on our servers.
  • Database Infrastructure: All applicant records and member card entries are securely encrypted and stored with Supabase.

4. Security & Data Retention

We implement industry-standard database rules, SSL encryption, and strict role-based access to safeguard your information from unauthorized access. We retain registration records as long as your membership is active in our student startup network, or until you request account deletion.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our platform uses privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (such as Vercel Analytics) solely to aggregate page performance, error telemetry, and geographic traffic trends. We do not profile you across third-party websites or deploy tracking pixels.

6. Contact & Rights

Under applicable Indian digital data protection standards, you have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal records. For any inquiries, please write to our privacy officer at support@thefuturecouncil.in.
