Privacy & Data Security

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly when you register for a chapter, apply for the Launchpad Fellowship, or subscribe to Startup School. This includes your name, email address, WhatsApp telephone number, college affiliation, academic major, year of study, and startup project concepts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Review and process your fellowship application and provide individualized written feedback.
  • Coordinate campus chapter build nights and match co-founder candidates.
  • Deliver cohort educational content and facilitate mentor office hours.
  • Facilitate direct intros to vetted angel investors (with your explicit consent).

We never sell your personal data to external advertisers, recruiters, or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

All applicant data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption and stored securely in dedicated cloud infrastructure (Supabase with row-level security). Access to unredacted startup applications is restricted strictly to the founding partners and assigned mentor reviewers.

4. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request a full copy of the data we hold regarding your profile or request complete deletion of your records from our systems at any time by emailing us at support@thefuturecouncil.in.
