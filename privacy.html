Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you apply for our fellowship program or register for community updates. This information includes:

  • Personal identifiers: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Professional/Educational details: College affiliation, course, and year of study.
  • Project details: Startup descriptions, slide decks, vertical domains, and team compositions.
  • Financial Aid details: Case statements detailing request grounds.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Evaluate project applications for the Fellowship cohort.
  • Log registration inputs and manage records in our Supabase databases.
  • Contact you via Email or WhatsApp/SMS regarding your application status, interview schedules, and program updates.
  • Assess requests for financial support or residency scholarships.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Payments

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share information with:

- **Razorpay**: Fellowship application payments are processed through Razorpay's secure checkout. When you pay, you are redirected to Razorpay, which collects your transaction details, contact inputs, and payment mode data according to its own privacy protocols.

- **Supabase**: Your form submissions are saved securely on cloud servers managed by Supabase.

4. Security & Data Retention

We implement industry-standard database rules and access keys to keep your records safe from unauthorized access. We retain registration records for as long as necessary to administer our network databases, alumni indexes, and communication campaigns, unless deletion is requested.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses basic analytics (such as Vercel Analytics) to track page views, bounce rates, and browser regions. We do not track you across other websites or deploy profiling scripts.

6. Contact Details

If you wish to access, correct, or request deletion of your information, please contact us at **contact@thefuturecouncil.in**.
