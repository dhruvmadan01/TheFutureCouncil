Fellowship '26

Launching our very first startup cohort for 20 select student teams.

We operate through local campus branches in different colleges, connecting elite student builders. Fellowship '26 brings together 20 high-potential startup squads from our member societies across Delhi University and beyond to build, validate, and scale.

FELLOWSHIP FEE & LOCATION REQUIREMENTS

📍 Delhi NCR Residency

Fellowship '26 is an offline residency. You must be present in Delhi NCR or ready to travel. We provide support for accommodation.

💰 Fee: ₹1.5 Lakhs

Need-blind admissions. We offer extensive scholarship packages and accommodation assistance for deserving squads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply to the Fellowship?

Any student-led startup team containing at least one member currently enrolled in an accredited college or university. We prioritize teams represented in our member societies and campus branches, but independent student startups are highly welcome to apply!

What is the fee and is financial aid available?

The program fee for Fellowship '26 is ₹1.5 Lakhs. However, we offer 100% need-blind admissions and extensive scholarships. Accommodation assistance is provided for founders traveling to Delhi NCR.

Can solo founders apply?

Yes, solo founders can apply. However, we strongly encourage you to find developers, designers, or growth co-founders using our **Council OS Live Talent Radar** first, build your team, and then apply for the fellowship as a squad.

What if we do not have an active society branch?

You can still apply! If your college doesn't have an active chapter of The Future Council, you can also use your application to express interest in launching a local branch at your campus (similar to starting a local student organization chapter).
